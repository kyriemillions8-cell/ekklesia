What Is Ekklesia Youth?

A generation after God's heart — not silent, not asleep.

Ekklesia Youth is a Christian evangelical movement in Botswana, founded in 2026, with a vision to see young people encounter Jesus Christ, become rooted in the Bible, and live out their faith with courage, humility, holiness and love.

We are building a community where people can learn Scripture, pray together, grow as disciples, encourage one another and share the good news of Jesus.

Our desire is to see a generation shaped by God's heart and ready to serve their communities and the world. Through Bible study, prayer, evangelism, discipleship, fellowship and service, we want to help people grow in their walk with Christ.

Whether you are exploring Christianity, returning to faith, or seeking a community in which to grow, Ekklesia Youth invites you to discover Jesus and walk with us.

The Heart of Our Message

The good news changes everything.

Christianity begins with Jesus Christ. This is the good news we want every person to hear, understand and respond to.

01

Our Need

Every person has sinned and needs reconciliation with God. We cannot save ourselves through good works or human effort.

Romans 3:23

02

God's Answer

God showed His love through Jesus Christ. Jesus died for our sins and rose again, offering forgiveness and new life to everyone who believes in Him.

John 3:16 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

03

Our Response

Turn to God, place your faith in Jesus, receive His grace and follow Him. Grow through prayer, Scripture, fellowship and obedience.

Romans 10:9–13

Would you like to know Jesus?

You do not have to have every answer before reaching out. Ask questions, request prayer or speak with our leadership team.
